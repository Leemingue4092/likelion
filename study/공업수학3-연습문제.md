## A. 유형

1. \(\displaystyle {dy \over dx}+{3 \over x}y=0\)
2. \(\displaystyle {dy \over dx}+{3 \over x}y=x^{2}\)
3. \(\displaystyle {dy \over dx}+y=e^{-x}y^{2}\)
4. \((2xy+3)\,dx+x^{2}\,dy=0\)
5. \(\displaystyle {dy \over dx}={x^{2}+y^{2} \over xy}\)
6. \(\displaystyle {dy \over dx}=x(1-y^{2})\)
7. \(\displaystyle {dy \over dx}+2y=2y^{2}\)
8. \((y\cos x+2x)\,dx+\sin x\,dy=0\)

## B. 적분

1. \(\displaystyle \int_{0}^{4} x\sqrt{x^{2}+9}\,dx\)
2. \(\displaystyle \int_{0}^{1} {dx \over x^{2}+3x+2}\)
3. \(\displaystyle \int {x \over 1-x^{4}}\,dx\)
4. \(\displaystyle \int {4x+2 \over (x^{2}+1)(x^{2}+4)}\,dx\)

## C. 미분방정식

1. \(\displaystyle {dy \over dx}=(x+2)y^{2}\)
2. \(\displaystyle {dy \over dx}=2xy(y-1),\quad y(0)=2\)
3. \(y'+2xy=0,\quad y(0)=5\)
4. \(y'-y\sin x=0,\quad y(0)=2\)
5. \(y'+2y=4x\)
6. \(y'-y=e^{2x},\quad y(0)=1\)
7. \(y'+y=\sin x\)
8. \(y'+y=e^{x}y^{2}\)
9. \(y'+2y=2y^{2}\)
10. \((2xy+3)\,dx+x^{2}\,dy=0\)
11. \((y\cos x+2x)\,dx+\sin x\,dy=0\)
12. \(\displaystyle {dy \over dx}=1+{y \over x}\)
